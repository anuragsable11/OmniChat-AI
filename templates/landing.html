How it works

From question to answer in four steps.

A simple, resilient pipeline that keeps heavy work away from your browser and your data inside your walls.

01

You ask

Your message is stored instantly through the Django API and the request returns right away.

POST /api/chat/
02

It's queued

A Celery worker picks up the job together with recent conversation context from Redis.

generate_ai_response_task
03

The model thinks

Qwen3 generates a reply through Ollama, entirely on your own hardware.

ollama · qwen3:4b
04

You get the answer

The finished reply is pushed straight to your screen over an authenticated WebSocket.
